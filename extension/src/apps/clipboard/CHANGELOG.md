# Changelog

## 1.1.3 — 2026-07-27

- Make the in-page surfaces follow the Appearance setting

## 1.1.2 — 2026-07-26

- The clipboard picker, the field badge and the copy confirmation follow your Appearance
  setting instead of always being dark.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-07-22

- Copied colours in every modern CSS form — 4- and 8-digit hex with alpha, oklch(),
  lab(), lch(), hwb(), color() — are now classified as colours, not text. Colours
  already saved as text are corrected the next time the app opens.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-07-22

- Deleting no longer removes favorites on a plain confirm: the confirm says how many of
  them are favorites and asks you to acknowledge those explicitly. Applies to the batch
  bar and to a single row alike, so Select all can never quietly take your favorites.
- Favorites keep their star while multi-selecting, so you can see what you are deleting.

## 1.0.1 — 2026-07-22

- Deleting one clip now asks first and offers Undo, instead of deleting on the first click.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-11

- Initial release.
