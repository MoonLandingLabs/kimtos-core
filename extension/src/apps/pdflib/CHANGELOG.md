# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-25

- Adding a PDF by URL was removed, so PDF Library makes no network request. Upload PDFs from your computer instead; books you added by URL before stay in your library.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-08-12

- Download a book you already added, from its row in the reading list or from the reader toolbar. It saves the stored PDF as it was imported or uploaded, named after the book's title; the book, its highlights and its reading progress stay where they are.

## 1.1.1 — 2026-07-22

- Removing one book now offers Undo, like removing several does.

## 1.1.0 — 2026-07-21

- Make the all-hosts grant optional and per-feature

## 1.0.0 — 2026-07-06

- Initial release.
