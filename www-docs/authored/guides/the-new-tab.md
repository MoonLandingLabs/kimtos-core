# The new tab

Everything outside the apps themselves: the dock, the search bar, the wallpaper, the home
cards, and where the settings actually are.

## The dock

Your installed apps sit along the bottom. Click one to open it.

- **Clicking a running app closes it.** The dock is a toggle, not a taskbar — if a window
  is already open, clicking its icon puts it away.
- **Right-click a tile** for two options: **Open**, and **Pin to the left** (or **Unpin
  from the dock**). Pinned apps stay in a fixed cluster on the left; the rest scroll.
- A small **badge** shows a count on some apps: open tasks on Tasks, unread books on PDF
  Library.

Apps you have not installed are not in the dock. Add them from the **App Store** — the
grid button, top right.

## The search bar

Focused automatically on every new tab, so you can just start typing. Enter a search, or
paste a URL to go straight there.

Searches go to **your browser's own search engine** — whichever one you have set in Chrome.
Kimtos does not choose one for you and has no engine setting of its own, so changing your
default in Chrome changes what this bar does. Paste a full URL (one starting `http://` or
`https://`) and it goes straight there instead of searching; anything else is a search, so
typing `package.json` looks it up rather than guessing at a website.

The bar has a second mode. In **Settings ▸ Preferences ▸ Search input**, choose **KimtAI**
instead of **Web search**, and the bar becomes _"Ask KimtAI anything…"_ — whatever you
type opens the AI chat with your question already sent.

## Wallpaper

The bar in the bottom right has three buttons: **Shuffle wallpaper**, **Favorite this
wallpaper** (the star), and **Toggle full screen**.

By default the wallpaper follows your theme — a light one in light mode, a dark one in
dark mode. It does **not** change on every new tab. Press **Shuffle** to change it, or
**★ Favorite** one to pin it so it stays.

**Settings ▸ Wallpaper** has the gallery. You can **Upload a wallpaper** of your own (up
to {{wallpaperMaxSize}}; it is cropped to fit your screen and pinned automatically), delete any you have
added, or **Restore default wallpapers** to bring back the ones that ship with Kimtos.

To soften the wallpaper behind your text, use **Settings ▸ Personalization ▸ Background
blur**.

## The home cards

Optional cards above the dock. Each is switched on or off in **Settings ▸ Preferences**,
under **Home screen**:

| Card                 | What it shows                                                          | Setting                |
| -------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| **Today**            | Up to five open tasks, overdue first. Tick one to complete it.         | **Pin today's tasks**  |
| **Continue reading** | Your most recent PDFs — click to resume.                               | **Pin last read book** |
| **Notes**            | Recent collections, and a shortcut to make a new one.                  | **Pin notes**          |
| **Focus**            | A Pomodoro timer — 25, 15 or 5 minutes. Keeps running across new tabs. | **Pin focus timer**    |
| **Tips**             | The rotating hint line under the dock.                                 | **Show tips**          |

Two things worth knowing:

- **A card with nothing to show hides itself.** Turn on **Pin notes** before you have
  written any notes and you will see nothing — that is the card waiting, not a bug.

The KimtAI and NotaBoard cards appear automatically when those apps are installed.

## Where the settings are

**Click the gear button in the top right to open Settings.**

Six sections, and two of them are not where you would guess:

| Section             | What is in it                                                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Personalization** | Your display name, and the background blur slider                                          |
| **Wallpaper**       | The gallery, uploads, restore defaults                                                     |
| **Preferences**     | **The light/dark theme**, the search input mode, the clock, and all the home-card switches |
| **Apps**            | Per-app settings, grouped by app                                                           |
| **Data**            | Export and import your backup                                                              |
| **About**           | Version, report an issue                                                                   |

::: tip Looking for dark mode?
It is **Settings ▸ Preferences ▸ Appearance** — System, Light or Dark. Not under
Personalization, which is where most people look first.
:::

## Keyboard shortcuts

Kimtos is mostly mouse-driven, with one global shortcut and a few inside apps.

| Shortcut                | What it does                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Alt+Shift+V**         | Open your clipboard history to paste an earlier item — works on any page             |
| **Alt+Shift+1 / 2 / 3** | Paste the 1st, 2nd or 3rd most recent clip straight into the field you are typing in |
| **⌘/Ctrl+Enter**        | Submit, in Tasks, Rewordit and Thoth                                                 |
| **⌘/Ctrl+F**            | Find, inside Notes                                                                   |

The clipboard shortcuts need the **Clipboard** app installed.
