# Code of conduct

We want KimtOS to be a friendly place to contribute. Everyone taking part in issues, pull
requests and discussions agrees to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Welcome newcomers and help them get started.
- Give and accept feedback constructively.
- Avoid harassment, insults, discrimination and personal attacks of any kind.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads and block people who break these
rules. To report a problem, use the **Security** tab's private reporting or contact a
maintainer through the repository.
